The Hub

This Is Sacred Ground,
Not a Museum

The Urban Resilience Hub in Iowa City is the physical home of work led by the Great Plains Action Society — rooted in Indigenous knowledge, community care, and decolonial practice. Understanding what this place is — and is not — is the first step toward engaging with it well.

🌿

A Living Place

The Hub is where Indigenous-led work happens in real time. It is not a historical exhibit or a community center open to casual drop-in visits. Life, labor, and purpose unfold here on the community's terms.

🤝

Rooted in Relationship

Access to the Hub follows relationship. Those who have worked here did so over time — showing up with their labor, building trust, and earning a place within an ongoing community of effort.

⚠️

A Pattern to Unlearn

The assumption that a space is available to observe or learn from on one's own schedule is itself a settler-colonial pattern. Unlearning that reflex is part of what respectful engagement requires.
